Questions, answered

What do you need from me?

Start with your email and company. For the audit, we’ll request invoices, container numbers, and available tariff and event records. No ERP access needed.

How long does it take?

We aim to provide initial findings within 3 business days of receiving complete records. Carrier dispute and recovery timelines vary.

What if you find nothing?

You pay nothing. You still get a summary of what we checked.

How do you get paid?

The audit is free. Recovery work carries a 20% success fee with a $2,500 minimum, payable only after recovery. We agree the fee and scope with you before proceeding, including how the minimum affects smaller claims.

Is my data safe?

Ask for an NDA before sharing invoices. Contact us to agree how documents will be transferred and handled. Please don’t send sensitive documents through the enquiry form.
